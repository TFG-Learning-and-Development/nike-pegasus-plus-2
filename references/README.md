# Nike Pegasus Plus 2 source materials

- `nike-pegasus-plus-2-storyboard-v1.docx`: approved learner copy, sequence, activities, answers and feedback.
- `nike-pegasus-plus-2-mood-board.pdf`: colour, imagery and composition reference. Its appliance answers in the assessment mockup are placeholders; the storyboard supplies the actual answer options.

Both documents are unchanged copies of the supplied originals. Course images are copied or extracted under `public/assets/course/` with descriptive filenames. The Totalsports header mark is an SVG recreation of the mood-board wordmark, using its supplied brand styling. Unrelated Winflo and Quest product images from the mood board were excluded.
